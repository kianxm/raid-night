# 0006: Night critters theme

**Status:** accepted · 2026-10-27

**Decision.** Lock the "moonlit town of night critters" theme: you run a den of glowing
critters and send a raccoon crew to raid other dens after dark.

**Why.** Living collectibles drive attachment, naming, trading and loss aversion. Night
lighting is cheap to make look good on Roblox and makes rare critters pop in clips. It gives
"Raid Night" a literal meaning.

**Still to test.** Icon and thumbnail click-through with 2-3 mockups before launch art is
final. If the theme tests poorly, the async raid core works with any theme (alternatives in
the full design doc).
